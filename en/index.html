BEFORE WE TALK

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in a quotation request?

Equipment type and model if available, fault description, site location and nameplate photos. Photos can be attached in WhatsApp.

How are assessment fees and timing agreed?

They are agreed before the visit after reviewing your request, equipment location and assessment scope.

Can I request team training?

Yes. Share the topics, participant numbers, experience level and training location to define a proposed programme.

How do I register as an engineer or technician?

Choose Careers / collaboration in the form and share your specialism, experience and location. Attach your CV in WhatsApp or email.

Plan for safe execution

During scoping, we review permits, energy isolation, equipment access and required protective equipment with the site contact. Execution follows site procedures and the agreed scope.
