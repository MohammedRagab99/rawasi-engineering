Privacy & contact

This site does not save form submissions to a database. Your entries are used in your browser to prepare a WhatsApp message or email. You choose whether to send that message in the external app.

WhatsApp, your email provider, Google Fonts and GitHub Pages process connection data under their own policies. This version includes no analytics scripts.

Send only information needed to assess the request. Avoid confidential site documents in the initial message. For questions about information you have sent, contact mohammed.ragab@eng.asi.edu.eg.
